What Ownio is for

Photographing your possessions, recording what they are and roughly what they are worth, and producing a dated report you can give to an insurer, a landlord, a loss adjuster or anyone else who asks you to prove what you had. It is a documentation tool. What anyone does with that document — accept it, question it, pay against it — is between you and them.

The estimates

  • Values are produced by an AI model reading a photograph. It can be wrong about what an object is, what brand it is, and what it is worth.
  • Every field is editable, and the estimate a scan produces is a first draft for you to correct. Items the model was unsure about are marked Worth checking.
  • You are responsible for what is in the report you send. Check it before you rely on it.
  • For jewellery, art, watches and anything else of substantial value, get a professional appraisal. Many policies sub-limit those categories and will not pay the documented figure without one.
  • Ownio does not convert between currencies. The figures are in whichever currency you selected, and changing that setting changes the symbol, not the numbers.
